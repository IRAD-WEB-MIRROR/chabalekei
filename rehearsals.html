Where

The rehearsal room on the second floor of the Palace of Art, Cathedral Street 4. Saturday starters sing in the concert hall. Use the side door on the park side; the guard knows the children.

Before the concerts

In the two weeks before a concert all three groups rehearse together on Sunday at 14:00 as well. We send a note home.

Holidays

No rehearsals in the school holidays, except in the week before Christmas. The summer camp at the end of June is for juniors and seniors.

Teachers

Gulnara Sydykova, conductor (“Gulnara Eje” to the children). Ainura Bekova, piano. Mira Pak, starters.
